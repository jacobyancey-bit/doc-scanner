## 1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy
At Dangling Dice Studios, we believe your personal documents belong solely to you. **DocScanner** is engineered with a strict **privacy-first, on-device architecture**. All document scanning, edge boundary detection, perspective cropping, image enhancement, and PDF creation take place entirely on your device. We do not operate tracking servers or harvest document contents.

## 2. Information We Collect (or Rather, Do Not Collect)
- **Document Contents & Scanned Images:** All photos taken with the scanner and all compiled PDF files remain strictly within your device's local application sandbox storage. We never upload, view, or process your documents on our servers.
- **Personal Data:** We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide your name, phone number, or email address to use the core features of the app.
- **Biometric or Facial Data:** We do not collect or analyze biometric data from photos or identity documents.
- **Analytics & Third-Party Trackers:** DocScanner does not contain third-party ad networks, behavioural tracking SDKs, or invasive telemetry tools.

## 3. Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed
DocScanner requests minimal Android operating system permissions necessary to function:
- **`android.permission.CAMERA`:** Used strictly in real time to capture images of physical papers, receipts, or whiteboards via Google Play Services ML Kit. Camera frames are processed on-device and never saved or transmitted elsewhere without your direct action.
- **`android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`:** Used solely when you instruct the app to export or save a generated PDF document to your device's public Downloads/Documents directory.
- **`android.permission.INTERNET`:** Used only if you choose to initiate optional web pairing via QR code or use the Android system share sheet to send a PDF via your personal email, cloud drive, or messaging apps.

## 4. Optional Web Synchronization
DocScanner includes an optional web pairing feature (`docscan://`). If you choose to scan a QR code on `https://dangling-dice-studios.com` (or an authorized partner service), the app sends the selected PDF directly to the session endpoint you authorized via encrypted HTTPS. This transfer occurs only upon your explicit action.

## 5. Children’s Privacy
DocScanner does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is a general utility designed for all audiences.
